About CampusFind

CampusFind is a campus-based Lost and Found platform designed to help students report, search and recover lost items quickly and securely.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make the process of finding lost items on campus simple, organized and accessible to every student.

For Students

CampusFind provides students with a centralized place to report lost items and share information about items they have found.

Simple and Secure

User accounts and organized reports help keep the campus Lost and Found process structured and secure.

How CampusFind Works

1

Report

Report an item you have lost or found on campus.

2

Search

Search the available Lost and Found reports using useful filters.

3

Match

CampusFind identifies possible matches between lost and found reports.

4

Recover

Students can use the available information to recover their lost belongings.
